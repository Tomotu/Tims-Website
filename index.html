About

I'm Tim Chong, a photographer based in Singapore. My work covers portraits, sports, events, and nightlife — I'm drawn to unguarded moments, the kind that happen between the posed ones.

Whether it's a padel court at golden hour or a party at 2am, the approach is the same: stay ready, stay quiet, let the scene come to you.
